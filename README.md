# ☕ Cafe Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive business intelligence dashboard built with **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze cafe sales performance, product revenue, transaction activity, payment methods, and sales trends.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project transforms raw cafe sales data into an interactive and business-focused analytics dashboard.

The dashboard was designed from a **cafe owner's perspective**, focusing on the key information required to understand business performance, identify sales trends, evaluate product performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

The final dashboard consists of four interactive sections:

- **Overview**
- **Sales**
- **Products**
- **Transactions**

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A cafe generates a large amount of sales data, but raw transaction records alone do not provide an easy way to understand business performance.

The key challenge is to transform raw data into meaningful information that can answer questions such as:

- How is the cafe performing overall?
- Which months generate the highest and lowest revenue?
- Which products contribute the most revenue?
- Which products are performing poorly?
- How do in-store and takeaway sales compare?
- Which payment methods contribute the most revenue?
- What important patterns can be identified from the sales data?

This project addresses these questions through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

The project followed a structured analytics workflow:

### 1. Data Understanding

The raw cafe sales dataset was examined to understand:

- Available fields
- Data types
- Sales information
- Product information

- Location information
- Payment methods

### 2. Data Cleaning & Transformation

The data was prepared using **Microsoft Excel and Power Query for Excel**.

The preparation process included:

- Identifying and handling missing values
- Removing unnecessary or blank records
- Checking data consistency

- Preparing fields for analysis
- Structuring the dataset for visualization

### 3. SQL Analysis

SQL was used to query, analyze, and validate the dataset.

The analysis focused on areas such as:

- Revenue
- Transactions
- Product performance

- Sales trends
- Business-level metrics

### 4. Data Modeling & Calculations

The prepared data was brought into Power BI.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was designed with four main sections:

- Overview

### 6. Business Analysis

The final dashboard was analyzed from a business perspective to identify:

- Sales trends

# 📊 Dashboard

## 1. Overview

The Overview page provides a high-level summary of the cafe's overall performance.

### Key Metrics

- Total Revenue
- Total Transactions
- Quantity Sold
- Items Sold

### Analysis Includes

- Monthly revenue performance
- Revenue by product
- Revenue by location
- Revenue by payment method
- Overall business takeaways

The purpose of this page is to allow a business owner to understand the overall situation quickly without going through every individual chart.

---

## 2. Sales

The Sales page focuses specifically on sales performance and revenue trends.

### Key Metrics

- Total Revenue
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Transactions
- Average Revenue per Transaction

### Analysis Includes

- Monthly sales trend
- Highest-performing month
- Lowest-performing month
- Sales by location
- Key sales insights

The page provides a deeper understanding of when and where sales are being generated.

---

## 3. Products

The Products page focuses on product-level performance.

### Analysis Includes

- Revenue by product
- Top-performing products
- Lowest-performing products
- Product revenue contribution
- Product performance insights

The main purpose is to identify which products contribute most to the cafe's revenue and which products may require further attention.

---

## 4. Transactions

The Transactions page focuses on transaction activity and payment behavior.

### Key Metrics

- Total Transactions
- Total Revenue
- Average Revenue per Transaction

### Analysis Includes

- Revenue by payment method
- Cash performance
- Credit Card performance
- Digital Wallet performance
- Transaction insights

This page helps understand how customers are completing purchases and whether the business is dependent on a particular payment method.

---

# 🔍 Key Insights

The analysis identified several important business insights:

- Total revenue generated was approximately **80.48K**.
- Approximately **10K transactions** were recorded.
- Approximately **29K quantities** were sold.
- **June** recorded the highest monthly revenue.
- **February** recorded the lowest monthly revenue.
- **Salad** generated the highest product revenue at approximately **15.6K**.
- **Sandwich** and **Smoothie** were among the strongest-performing products.
- **In-store revenue** was slightly higher than takeaway revenue.
- **Cash, Credit Card, and Digital Wallet** showed relatively balanced performance.
